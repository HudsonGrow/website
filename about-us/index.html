Purpose

Hudson Grow was created with the intention of: 

1.  Connecting adults to the Hudson Valley’s unique cannabis offerings. 

2.  Fostering a community of stewardship among those who enjoy cannabis culture.

3.  Give back in the name of environmental conservation.  Protect the Hudson Valley.
